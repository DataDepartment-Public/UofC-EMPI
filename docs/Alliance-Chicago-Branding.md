# AllianceChicago — Branding Reference

> Source: [alliancechicago.org](https://alliancechicago.org/) — collected 2026-06-28. Visual values (colors, fonts) extracted from the site's stylesheets; messaging quoted from the site copy.

## Identity

- **Name:** AllianceChicago (legal/long form: *Alliance of Chicago Community Health Services*)
- **Type:** Dynamic, innovation-driven non-profit
- **Tagline(s):**
  - "Innovating for better health"
  - "Advancing health through collaboration, technology and research"

## Mission, Vision & Values

- **Mission:** "Improving personal, community and public health through innovative collaboration"
- **Mission (descriptive):** "A dynamic, innovation-driven non-profit dedicated to achieving health equity for vulnerable populations."
- **Vision / self-description:** "Fully-committed and deeply passionate about our role as a vital resource for community healthcare, focusing on care delivery transformation, technology, data and analytics, and research and evaluation."
- **Values:** "Diverse talents, shared values and a single focus" on improving public health through collaborative innovation. (Specific value list is not published on the site.)

## Key Messaging

- "Harnessing the power of collaboration across a national network of Community Health Centers"
- "Constantly implementing the latest healthcare technology innovations"
- Focus pillars: **quality, efficiency, experience, and outcomes**
- Help community health centers transform healthcare delivery through **data analytics and health information technology**

## What They Do (Service Areas)

- athenahealth solutions
- Care Delivery Transformation
- Data & Analytics
- Health Information Technology
- Research & Evaluation

**Solutions / products:** ACRES Analytics, athenaOne, athenaPractice, Sphygmo, technology partnerships

## Tone of Voice

Professional, innovation-focused, collaborative, mission-driven, and results-oriented. Emphasis on health equity, partnership, and technology-enabled outcomes.

## Visual Identity

### Logo
"AllianceChicago Enterprise REV Logo" used in the site header. Wordmark-based; "Alliance" and "Chicago" set as a single combined word.

### Typography
Loaded via Google Fonts (`display=swap`):

- **Montserrat** — primary typeface (weights 400/500/600/700/800/900); used for the large majority of text and headings.
- **Raleway** — secondary typeface (weights 400/500).
- **Astra** — fallback/system stack from the Astra WordPress theme (the site's CMS theme).
